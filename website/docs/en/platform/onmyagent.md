# OnMyAgent: Local Desktop Workspace

The primary desktop product for sessions, files, Experts and Skills, Automation, approvals, and BYOK.


## 1. What you do here

- Assign and follow up on office tasks
- Manage workspace source material and deliverables
- Install Experts and Skills and connect external tools
- Configure models, your personal profile, global preferences, system permissions, and the local environment; environment settings are part of the System section
